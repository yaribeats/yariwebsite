# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Independent artists looking for distinctive production, a collaborator who understands their sound, and a direct way to start a serious project.

## Product Purpose

YARI.WTF is a producer portfolio that turns a visitor's first listen into a production conversation. Success means artists quickly understand Yari's taste, hear the range in the current beats, and book a production call.

## Brand Personality

Restless, personal, and sharp. The voice is confident without sounding corporate, playful without becoming unserious, and direct enough to feel like Yari is already in the room.

## Anti-references

Generic beat-store grids, luxury black-and-gold producer templates, sterile SaaS landing pages, magazine-style serif layouts, glassmorphism, and motion that gets between the listener and the music.

## Design Principles

- Let the work speak first: music, video, and photography carry the story.
- Make every route feel like part of one visual mixtape.
- Keep the conversion direct: listening should naturally lead to booking.
- Use personality as proof, not decoration.
- Respect the visitor's attention and device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, descriptive media labels, no audio autoplay, captions already present in source video where available, and reduced-motion fallbacks for every animated behavior.
